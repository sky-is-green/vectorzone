# M1 — Block tank: learning notes

## three.js concepts used

- **Groups & transforms**: `hull.group` carries position + heading; `turret.group` is a *child* at the mount pivot with its own yaw/pitch. Parent/child transforms compose — the turret inherits hull motion for free. This is the scene-graph idea that makes articulated bodies trivial.
- **BufferGeometry by hand**: the merged builder pushes raw float arrays (positions) into `Float32BufferAttribute`. No loaders, no models — geometry is *computed* from the block grid. Faces = triangle soup (2 tris per exposed face); edges = `LineSegments` pairs.
- **Interior culling**: a face is emitted only where the neighbour cell is empty. Fewer triangles, correct silhouettes, and — the payoff — when M3 removes blocks, the newly exposed interior faces appear automatically on rebuild. Blow-through is a *consequence* of this rule, not a special effect.
- **Edge dedup**: adjacent exposed faces share edges; a Map keyed by sorted endpoint coordinates emits each line once.
- **MeshBasicMaterial, DoubleSide, black**: the occluder. Unlit, writes depth, hides what's behind. `LineBasicMaterial` green draws the light. Two materials, reused across every body.
- **Geometry lifecycle**: `dispose()` old geometries on rebuild; rebuild triggered only by `grid.rev` change (invariant I10), never per frame.
- **Camera rig math**: eye = tank pos + yaw-rotated offset above/behind the turret; look direction = (sin·cos, sin, cos·cos) of yaw/pitch. First-person "rides the turret" falls out of composing heading + turretYaw.

## Sim concepts used

- **Block grid as data**: indexed cells, `rev` mutation counter, layouts as readable string layers (`parseLayout`) — hand-authored, diffable, tunable. Same representation will drive structures in M3.
- **Layout = stats**: mass from block types → top speed/accel; tread count → turn rate. The canonical tank is ~150 blocks; formulas are simple and recorded in `tank.ts`.
- **Commands, not inputs**: the sim consumes `TankCommand`; keyboard is just one producer. AI (M5) and network (M7) produce the identical shape.
- **Fixed-tick kinematics**: accel-limited speed approach, yaw rate, independent turret slew, clamped pitch, arena clamp. All pure functions of (state, command, dt).

## Decisions & deviations

- **Deviation (flagged):** PLAN §4.2 originally said "2 draw calls per tank"; the locked independent-turret controls make that impossible (a rotating body can't share static geometry). Resolved to **2 draw calls per rigid body → 4 per tank**; PLAN §4.2/§12 and BUILD M1-A3 updated accordingly. Still two orders of magnitude under the 100-call gate.
- Camera eye raised above the turret roof (first attempt sat below it and the periscope view was blocked by our own turret — caught in browser verification).
- Added `window.__vz` dev hook (`world`, `renderer`, `debug.cam`) for browser probes and screenshots. Dev-only; the sim never reads it.
- Turret pitch currently tilts the whole turret sub-grid; barrel-only pitch is a possible later refinement (noted, not needed for acceptance).
