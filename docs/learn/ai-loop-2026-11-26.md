# AI loop overhaul — 2026-11-26

User observation: AI wobbled with info, stopped without info, and did not act
on scan/gunfire contacts.

## New state machine

`PATROL → INVESTIGATE → ENGAGE → PATROL`

- No contacts: choose a waypoint, drive at 55% throttle, scan turret slowly.
- Contact without LOS: drive to last-known position, search nearby, never fire.
- Visual confirmation: hold a range band (no fixed-point orbit), aim hull, fire.
- Target dead/contact expired: clear and repeat.
- Reposition is only stuck recovery.

## Wobble fixes

- Removed orbit-point movement that continuously circled a fixed point.
- Range hold uses dead zones; in-band tanks stop rather than jitter.
- Stuck detection measures displacement over a perception interval, not per tick.
- Patrol/investigate goals persist until reached; no target snapping.

## Combat maneuvers (follow-up)

- In-band tanks no longer stop: they strafe and reverse direction every 2–4 s.
- Too far → advance; too close → retreat.
- `World.threats` records enemy shots passing within 3.5 m; AI evades for 1.5 s.
- Turret aim/fire is independent of hull movement.

## Cover-seeking follow-up

- Replaced strafing with a throttled cover search: candidate positions beside
  nearby structures must have LOS to the target and remain in the range band.
- No cover → persistent smooth orbit angle around the target.
- Incoming fire makes cover search urgent; no cover means continued orbit.

## Outnumbered follow-up (superseded)

- `World.targeters` tracks AI targets plus recent incoming-fire sources.
- 2+ targeters → closest attacker locked, flee goal biased away from enemies,
  fire continues while moving; no target flicker.

## Decision-model correction

The first outnumbered implementation was flawed: it read every AI brain's
current target, which leaked private intent and made nearly every four-way FFA
tank permanently "outnumbered." It also chose the closest known contact, not
the closest visible attacker, so a hidden ping could suppress fire on a visible
enemy.

Corrections:

- `World.incomingFire` records observed near-miss/hit sources per attacker.
- Visible threats outrank hidden memories; target switches use a 12 m margin
  and a one-second settle window.
- Pressure overrides movement even while investigating, but never firing rules.
- Cover candidates are sampled around structure perimeters, rejected if the
  firing line is blocked, and rejected if they do not interrupt a hull shot.
- Failed cover/flee searches are cached and throttled.
- Visible target motion gets imperfect shell-flight lead; error widens with
  lateral speed.

no fire through cover, hull aim, self-destruct. Browser confirmed all three
states. 152.0 KB gz, zero console errors.

Outnumbered follow-up: 133 tests; browser confirmed closest-attacker lock and

Decision-model correction: 141 tests; browser confirmed visible-target
retention, closest-visible pressure target, imperfect lead, protected cover,
cached failed searches, and combat escape movement; 153.9 KB gz, zero console
errors. Five-seed 30 s FFA probe reduced permanent outnumbered time from
1775/1800 ticks to 0–511/1800, but target-switch counts still vary by seed, so
human playtesting remains necessary.
