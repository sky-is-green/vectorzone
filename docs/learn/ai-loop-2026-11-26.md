# AI loop overhaul — 2026-11-26

User observation: AI wobbled with info, stopped without info, and did not act
on scan/gunfire contacts.

## New state machine

`PATROL → INVESTIGATE → ENGAGE → PATROL`

- No contacts: choose a waypoint, drive at 55% throttle, scan turret slowly.
- Contact without LOS: drive to last-known position, search nearby, never fire.
- Visual confirmation: hold a range band (no fixed-point orbit), aim hull, fire.
- Target dead/contact expired: clear and repeat.
- Reposition is only stuck recovery.

## Wobble fixes

- Removed orbit-point movement that continuously circled a fixed point.
- Range hold uses dead zones; in-band tanks stop rather than jitter.
- Stuck detection measures displacement over a perception interval, not per tick.
- Patrol/investigate goals persist until reached; no target snapping.

## Combat maneuvers (follow-up)

- In-band tanks no longer stop: they strafe and reverse direction every 2–4 s.
- Too far → advance; too close → retreat.
- `World.threats` records enemy shots passing within 3.5 m; AI evades for 1.5 s.
- Turret aim/fire is independent of hull movement.

## Cover-seeking follow-up

- Replaced strafing with a throttled cover search: candidate positions beside
  nearby structures must have LOS to the target and remain in the range band.
- No cover → persistent smooth orbit angle around the target.
- Incoming fire makes cover search urgent; no cover means continued orbit.

## Outnumbered follow-up

- `World.targeters` tracks AI targets plus recent incoming-fire sources.
- 2+ targeters → closest attacker locked, flee goal biased away from enemies,
  fire continues while moving; no target flicker.
