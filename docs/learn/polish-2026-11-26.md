# Polish pass — 2026-11-26

User play-test feedback turned into code + regression tests.

## AI / director

- Maintains **four living light tanks**, staggered respawns.
- **FFA targeting**: non-tracker AI prefer other tanks; exactly one living AI
  is the designated player tracker.
- **Supertank** first spawns ~2.5 min, then every 2–3 min, with alert.
- **Awareness perf**: full LOS scans throttled to 10 Hz; proximity and gunfire
  remain per-tick. Four AI no longer make the sim heavy.

## Missiles

- Airborne spawn (11–16 m), missile-shaped wireframe (body/nose/fins/exhaust).
- ~60% guided with 0.45 rad/s limited turn; escapable.
- Player targets emit `MISSILE LOCK`; radar shows all missiles, locks pulse red.
- Full 3-D shot-down and impact checks.

## Volcano

- 10–16 projectiles per 10 s eruption, spread across 320 m.
- Blast radius 7 m / damage 6; every volcanic impact leaves a 6–8 m lava pool
  lasting 40 s (2 damage / 0.5 s); 35% also deposit rock cover.
- Dense wide steam/ash plume, longer particles, hot crater color.
- Lava rings moved to one thick-line mesh; particles only embers (prevents
  pool exhaustion when many pools are active).

## Aircraft

- Spawns on an edge line through its target; centre-target zero-direction bug
  fixed (random bearing when target is at 0,0).
- Minor target corrections (~0.15 rad/s), still avoidable.
- Generous 5.5 m horizontal / 3.5 m vertical wings hitbox.
- Kill produces airburst + BOMBER DOWN alert.

## Feedback / identity / radar / lines

- Screen shake only from own fire, close ground explosions, and actual nearby
  impacts. Distant bombs and AI firing no longer shake.
- Explosive depots red; scanner large blue reinforced (hp 6, pen cost 5).
- Radar uses live hull headings, actual aircraft position/heading, scanner blue
  diamond, missile arrows + lock pulse.
- `Line2` hierarchy: floor 1 px, tanks 2.4, structures 1.4, aircraft 1.8,
  horizon 1.1–1.2, trajectory 0.9.
