# M3 — Fire & destroy: learning notes

## Sim concepts used

- **Voxel DDA (Amanatides & Woo)**: one traversal serves shells and (later) AI
  line-of-sight. Axis-aligned rays are first-class (`tDelta = Infinity`);
  grid-local metres keep x/z centred, y from 0. Occupied cells yielded in
  entry order with distances, so a 2 m shell step can never tunnel past a cell.
- **Penetration budget**: paying `PEN_COST[type]` *destroys* the entered cell —
  that single rule is blow-through. Under-budget hits chip and stop. Splash
  chips 6-neighbours; ammo cells queue explosions; explosions damage by radius
  and queue further ammo → deterministic chain reactions.
- **Structural integrity = 6-connectivity flood-fill from anchors**
  (hull: core · turret: gun · structure: base/ground). Two physical truths fell
  out of the tests and are *correct*, not bugs:
  - **Lintel effect**: cutting a wall's base does not drop its top — the top
    course bridges horizontally to anchored columns.
  - **Ground anchors hold**: a vertical cut leaves two standing walls; only
    *unsupported* fragments fall (shoot out a whole course → the floor above
    drops). This is exactly ENVIRONMENTS' floor-by-floor collapse.
- **Chunks → rubble**: detached cells become tumbling chunks (seeded velocities),
  settle after 1.2 s into arena-wide rubble cover, capped at 400 (oldest crumbles).
- **Events are per-tick**: `world.events` resets each step. Consumers must drain
  every tick (the client queues across the accumulator loop); probes must
  accumulate across steps or they read stale emptiness.

## three.js concepts used

- Reused the merged face/edge builder for structures; rubble merged arena-wide
  (2 draw calls, rebuilt only on `rubbleRev`); chunks are per-chunk edge
  geometry synced add/remove against `world.chunks`.
- **Particle pool extended with explicit segment direction**: zero-velocity
  decals (scorch rings) need an orientation independent of motion, otherwise
  the trail segment collapses to an invisible point.
- Additive vertex-color fade doubles as scorch-ring decay (20 s).

## Decisions & deviations

- **Tuning note (feel, for play-test):** the muzzle sits at 1.2 m; walls are
  0.9 m and depots 0.6 m, so flat shots overfly low cover. Realistic and
  tactically interesting (cover blocks flat fire), but players must elevate
  down to hit low structures — verify in the M3 play-test session.
- Shell speed 120 m/s = 2 m/tick: multi-tick penetration is intentional; tests
  step time instead of assuming one-tick tunnels.
- `SHELL_DAMAGE` removed: paid entry destroys outright; hp matters for splash,
  explosions, and partial (under-budget) hits.

## Bugs caught during verification

1. Test expectations assumed uncentred grids and one-tick tunnels — fixed tests.
2. Two "missing destruction" scares were correct physics (lintel, ground anchors).
3. Stale per-tick event reads in probes — fixed by accumulating.
4. Unused import caught by `check:sim` typecheck.
